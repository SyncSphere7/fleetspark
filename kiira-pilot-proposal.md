- **24 buses** currently deployed in Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area
- **450-bus export order** from South Africa
- Target capacity of **500,000 vehicles/year by 2030**

...you need a fleet management system that matches your ambition.

**Current pain points (our assumptions — to be validated):**
1. **No real-time fleet visibility** — Where are your buses right now? Are they running on schedule?
2. **Battery uncertainty** — Which buses need charging? Which batteries are degrading?

4. **Operational data** — How far are your buses traveling? What's the cost comparison vs diesel?
5. **Reporting to stakeholders** — Government, investors, and export customers need data

FleetSpark solves all of these.

## What FleetSpark Does

### During the Pilot (90 Days)

**Module 1: Live Fleet Dashboard**
- See all 24 buses on a real-time map
- Know which bus is in transit, charging, idle, or needs attention
- No manual check-ins required

**Module 2: Battery Health Monitoring**
- Track State of Health (SoH) for every bus battery
- Predict when batteries need replacement
- Monitor charging sessions and energy consumption
- Get alerts when battery drops below your threshold

**Module 3: Trip & Route Tracking**
- Automatic trip detection (start/stop)
- Distance, duration, and speed per trip
- Route visualization on the map
- Historical trip data export

**Module 4: Maintenance Scheduling**
- Schedule preventive maintenance by date or odometer
- Maintenance history per bus
- Alerts when service is due

**Module 5: Alerts & Notifications**
- Low battery alerts
- Vehicle offline alerts
- Maintenance due alerts
- Configurable notification channels (email, SMS, in-app)

**Module 6: Reports & Analytics**
- Fleet utilization (% of time buses are active)
- Total distance covered
- Estimated fuel cost savings vs diesel buses
- CO₂ emissions reduced
- Export-ready PDF and CSV reports

### What the Dashboard Looks Like

*A clean, simple web application accessible from any device:*
- Desktop (full dashboard with map + vehicle list)
- Tablet (optimized layout)
- Mobile phone (essential info at a glance)

No app installation required — runs in the browser.
